See any language, in place.

Polyglass is a transparent overlay for Windows that reads the text on your screen and draws the translation, in the language you choose, right over it. Spanish, Japanese, English and more. It works on anything on your screen, and everything stays on your PC.

pol·y·glass /ˈpɒl.i.ɡlæs/ noun

Etymology

A double entendre:

  • Poly- as in polyglot, from Greek polýglōttos, “many-tongued.” It speaks many languages.
  • Polly, as in the parrot. Parrots repeat what they hear, and so does this, just in another language.
  • -glass: the screen you’re looking through.

Glôssa (Greek, “tongue”) and glass are unrelated, but we’ll take it.

Definitions

  1. A see-through pane that speaks every language on your screen.
  2. (informal) The parrot that reads your supplier’s Chinese spreadsheet so you don’t have to paste it into a website.

“Don’t upload that report. Just polyglass it.”

What it does

Private by design

Your screen never leaves your PC. Reading and translating both run locally: no cloud, no account, nothing uploaded.

Works on anything on screen

Games, videos, PDFs, images, apps, web pages, remote desktops. If you can see it, it can translate it. No plugins or integrations.

Plug and play

No API keys, sign-ups or subscriptions. Internet is only needed once, for setup, and later only if you add a new language.

Translates in place

Each translation is painted over its source line, on a background colour sampled from your screen.

Any language, any direction

Pick From and To in the on-screen bar like Google Translate, or let it detect the language. New languages download with one click. Ctrl+Alt+D swaps them.

Many languages

Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Russian, Arabic, and Latin-script languages like Spanish, French and German, including stylised game fonts.

Live mode

Re-translates only when the text on screen actually changes, so it stays light on CPU.

Click-through

The overlay never gets in the way. Keep playing or working normally.

Install

  1. Download the ZIP and unzip it.
  2. Double-click Install.bat.
  3. Follow the setup wizard. Choose Translate from (or Detect language) and Translate to. It sets up Python, OCR language packs and offline translation models, and makes a desktop shortcut.

Needs an internet connection once, for setup, and about 1 GB of disk space. After that it runs offline. If Python 3.10–3.12 is missing, the installer gets it for you.

Uninstalling is just as easy: double-click Uninstall.bat. It removes the app's packages, the translation models (stored outside the app folder), the shortcut and your settings, then you delete the folder.

Use

Start Polyglass, click the window with the text, and press:

Ctrl+Alt+TTranslate the screen once
Ctrl+Alt+LTurn live mode on or off
Ctrl+Alt+DSwap the From and To languages
Ctrl+Alt+CClear the overlay
Ctrl+Alt+QQuit

Run games in windowed or borderless mode. Exclusive fullscreen draws above any overlay.
